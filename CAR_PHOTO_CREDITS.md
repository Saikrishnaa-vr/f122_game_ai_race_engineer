# Running-order car photo credits

The race dashboard requests small 2022 car photographs directly from Wikimedia Commons while the page is open. The photos are not bundled with this project. If the browser is offline or a request fails, the team-colour diamond remains visible. Each photo links to its Commons file page. The browser may cache requested images outside the D: project folder.

The dashboard displays the original Commons images as small thumbnails, cropped only by the browser's `object-fit: cover` presentation. Team and driver names identify the game's 2022 participant data; these photographs are illustrative and may show a different driver from the car currently occupying the running-order row.
